How do spending limits work?

All machines share one prepaid balance. A separate monthly compute cap defaults to $5; raising it does not add funds. Automatic recharge is optional and requires your authorization. See rates and a worked example.

What if I need persistent files?

Save a workspace before stopping to preserve its filesystem and restore it into a fresh container. Ordinary stop discards unsaved changes. Saved workspaces have account quotas and retention; keep independent exports. See saved workspaces.
