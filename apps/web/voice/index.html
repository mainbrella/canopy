Before you connect.

Can I hear it before setting anything up?

Yes. Call WE-MAIN-1010 (+1-936-246-1010) to try our live demo. To test your own business instructions, open the voice workspace and use browser voice or text.

What does self-hosting mean here?

Deploy the open-source backend and web app into your own Cloudflare account, configure authentication and billing, and connect your carrier. You operate that deployment and pay its bills. The application is GPLv3; Cloudflare and the hosted speech and inference models have their own terms. Read the setup guide.

Can I connect my existing phone system?

Inbound calls can use Twilio or Telnyx numbers. SIP support uses Twilio Programmable Voice SIP Domains or Telnyx TeXML application SIP subdomains to bridge your PBX to the agent. Mainbrella does not terminate SIP/RTP directly. See the Telnyx guide.

Is 2.8¢ per minute the hosted Mainbrella price?

No. It’s the rounded component-cost estimate for running your own deployment, using the three-minute Twilio example above. Hosted Mainbrella uses prepaid billing and its own rates. Self-hosted cost also depends on your workload, carrier tariffs, and infrastructure. See hosted billing.

Can it book appointments or transfer calls?

Not yet. Today’s agent answers from your business information and collects messages. Outbound calls, live transfers, calendar actions, and CRM updates need additional implementation.
